How the town grows

  • Each week your group meets its goal, the next piece of the current building goes up.
  • Signing in 3 or more times a week lights a streetlight.
  • Everyone in the group lives in town. The more thank-yous you share and receive, the busier your townsperson is.
  • Thank-yous to people outside the group bring visitors passing through.
  • Quiet weeks still add something small, like a tree or a flower bed. The town never goes backward.
