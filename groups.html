Delete ?

  • The town, its invite code, and all its notes of appreciation and visits are deleted for everyone in the group.

Delete your account?

  • Your sign-in, email, name and avatar are deleted.
  • The words of every note of appreciation you wrote are erased.
  • Your towns stay exactly as built. Your past notes and visits still count, without your name.
  • Any group you started passes to its longest-standing member, or is deleted if you're the only one in it.
  • Kid profiles on your account are removed the same way.

This can't be undone.
